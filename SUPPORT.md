# Support

VaneHub AI is community-supported: responses come from maintainers and contributors on a best-effort basis, with no service-level agreement.

Before filing anything, try the user guide's [troubleshooting chapter](docs/user-guide/en/src/troubleshooting.md) ([简体中文](docs/user-guide/zh-CN/src/troubleshooting.md)) and the [FAQ](docs/user-guide/en/src/faq.md).

**In scope**: reproducible defects in VaneHub AI itself, documentation gaps, and product proposals. **Out of scope**: the internal behavior of external CLIs and model providers (report those upstream), individual provider account or billing issues, and general prompt-engineering advice.

- Search [existing issues](https://github.com/cdavid817/vanehub-ai/issues) before opening a new one.
- Use the bug form for reproducible defects and the feature form for product proposals.
- Include the application version, operating system, relevant agent CLI versions, and minimal reproduction steps.
- Redact tokens, account details, local paths, conversation content, and other sensitive information from logs and screenshots.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately. Public issues are not an appropriate security-reporting channel.
